This policy explains what information laurawinslow.com collects and how it is used. The site is run by Laura Winslow, doing business as Watercolor Tales, in Carmel, Indiana.

## What we collect

- **Messages you send.** When you use the contact or wholesale form, we receive your name, email address and anything you write. Phone, business and website fields are optional.
- **Email signups.** If you join the mailing list, we keep your email address so we can send news about new work.
- **Basic site data.** Our web host may record standard server information, such as the pages requested and your browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

## How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, fill wholesale requests, and send the emails you signed up for. We do not sell or rent your information.

## Who else handles it

The site is hosted by Netlify, which also receives form submissions, including mailing list signups. Purchases happen on Etsy, Faire and Amazon, under their own privacy policies.

## Your choices

Every email includes a link to unsubscribe. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
